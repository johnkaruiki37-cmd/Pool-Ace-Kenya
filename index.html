About Us

Pool Ace was built on a simple idea: your pool deserves more than a quick scrub — it deserves expert care.

We treat every pool as what it truly is — a personal sanctuary. Whether it's a private home pool, an estate facility, or a shared residential pool, our team brings the same standard of professionalism, reliability, and attention to detail every time.

Today, Pool Ace proudly serves clients across Nairobi, Kiambu, Machakos (Kitengela), and Kajiado, offering flexible cleaning schedules and additional water-based services designed around your lifestyle.
